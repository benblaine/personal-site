About

Builder at heart,
community by nature

I grew up in the Helderberg region of the Western Cape, studied Mechanical Engineering at Stellenbosch University, and somehow ended up building some of South Africa's most-used tech products.

My journey started at FireID, an IT security company that became an incubator for ventures like Luno, SnapScan, JourneyApps, and OfferZen. I was on the founding teams for SnapScan and OfferZen, where I learned that the best products come from deeply understanding the people who use them.

Now I'm building Neurabuild, turning cutting-edge neuromorphic computing research into real-world products, and working with MoneyBadger on crypto payment infrastructure.
